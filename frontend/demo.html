About Us

Guru Tegh Bahadur Institute of Technology (GTBIT) was established in 1999 by the Delhi Sikh Gurdwara Management Committee (DSGMC), which runs a host of educational institutes and carries out numerous religious and social activities. GTBIT is a degree-level technical institute approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated to Guru Gobind Singh Indraprastha University, Delhi.
